JL vs JK vs TJ — What's Actually Different

Wrangler seat covers are not universal fit. Every generation has different seat geometry, bolster depth, headrest design, and airbag placement. A JK cover will not fit a JL and vice versa — even if the listing says "Wrangler."

  • JL (2018+): Deeper seat bolsters, updated airbag systems, JLU 4-door is the most popular fitment
  • JK (2007–2018): Flatter seat profile, 2-door and 4-door are significantly different
  • TJ (1997–2006): Simpler seat design, no side airbags, more universal options available
  • Gladiator JT: Truck seat shape — not interchangeable with Wrangler covers
