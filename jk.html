JK vs JKU Fitment

The JK (2-door) and JKU (4-door) have different rear seat configurations. The JKU has a full rear bench while the JK has a smaller fold-down rear. Do not order JKU covers for a 2-door JK. Specify "2-door" or "4-door" in your listing search. All Bartact JK covers are sold separately for 2-door and 4-door configurations.
