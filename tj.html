TJ Fitment Notes

The TJ Wrangler (1997–2006) does not have side-curtain airbags, which makes fitment simpler than JK or JL covers. The LJ Unlimited uses the same front seat dimensions as the standard TJ — TJ covers fit both. If you have a Sport, Sahara, or Rubicon trim, seat covers will fit the same — there are no trim-specific seat differences on the TJ.
